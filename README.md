# Claude Certified Associate Practice Sheets

**Your scorecard, miss log, an answer sheet for every set, the tie-break card, the trap list and a verdict with a date on it.**

The companion repo to the book *Claude Certified Associate Foundations Practice Questions*, from [youcanbuildthings.com](https://youcanbuildthings.com).

![Headline: Know by Day 14 if you are ready. A dark panel shows the same two mocks read two ways: by totals both clear 44 and look like go; by domain one domain sits under its line, so the verdict is re-check. Below it, every sheet by day with what you do on it: scorecard, miss log, diagnostic sheet, drill sheets, answer grid, trend table, tie-break card, trap list, Mocks 5 and 6, verdict sheet, exam-morning card. These are the blank sheets from the book, a folder per chapter, ready to print, so you write on paper instead of copying tables off a Kindle screen. Two terms turn up on them, both from Chapter 2. The *readiness line* is 44 of 60 on a mock: this book's own rule, not Anthropic's. The *deciding phrase* is the few words in a question that pick the answer. You get the blanks here; the 605 questions, their keys and every explanation stay in the book. Find your day below.

## Start here

### "I've just started the book"

It's Day 1. Print the [scorecard](chapters/02-scorecard-and-miss-log/scorecard.md) in landscape at 75% scale, the [miss log](chapters/02-scorecard-and-miss-log/miss-log.md) and the [diagnostic answer sheet](chapters/03-diagnostic/answer-sheet.md). Then sit Chapter 3's 35 questions in one 70-minute block, with the answer sheet beside the book.

### "I'm on the drills"

Days 2 to 7. Print the drill sheet for the domain your drill order puts next: [Domain 2](chapters/04-domain-2-drills/drill-sheet.md), [Domains 4 and 6](chapters/05-domains-4-and-6-drills/drill-sheet.md), [Domains 1 and 7](chapters/06-domains-1-and-7-drills/drill-sheet.md) or [Domains 3 and 5](chapters/07-domains-3-and-5-drills/drill-sheet.md).

### "I'm about to sit a mock"

Days 8 to 13. Print the [answer grid](chapters/08-mock-1/answer-grid.md) and write the mock's number at the top. Don't print that mock's score table until you've marked all 60: it shows which question belongs to which domain, and the mock keeps that from you until the key.

### "It's Day 14"

Print the [verdict sheet](chapters/12-verdict/verdict.md) and the [exam-morning card](chapters/12-verdict/exam-morning-card.md).

For everything at once, click the green **Code** button at the top of this page, then **Download ZIP**. For one sheet, open it here and print it from your browser.
