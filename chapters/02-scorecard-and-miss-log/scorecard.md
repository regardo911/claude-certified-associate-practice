## The Exam Facts Card

Check every line against the official exam guide and the FAQ before you rely on it. Anthropic can change any of them, and the guide is the document that wins.

- [ ] **60 questions · 120 minutes (about 135 minutes in the seat) · scaled score 100 to 1,000, pass at 720 · $99 before any partner discount · valid 12 months · multiple choice and multiple response, each question tells you how many to pick · online with a proctor or at a Pearson test center · closed book.**
- [ ] **Who may sit it:** staff at Claude Partner Network firms, registering with a company email on the partner's domain. A personal email won't work. You must be 18 or older with government ID.
- [ ] **What it costs:** $99 an attempt at list price. Partner firms at higher tiers get a discount, and many employers pay the fee outright.
- [ ] **Language:** the exam page lists English and Japanese, and you choose when you schedule.
- [ ] **Reschedule or cancel:** at least 48 hours before your appointment, or you lose the fee. The FAQ, the policies page and Pearson all say 48 hours; if your copy of the guide shows a shorter window, plan on 48 anyway.
- [ ] **Retakes:** 14 days after a first fail, 30 after a second, 90 after a third; up to four attempts in any rolling 12 months; every attempt is the full fee. A pass can't be retaken to raise the score.
- [ ] **Renewal:** a free, open-book online assessment before your expiry date; you can retake it as often as you need; let it lapse and you sit the full exam at the full fee.
- [ ] **Result:** on screen when you finish, with your domain percentages on the score report. The badge comes through Credly.
