# Chapter 2 · Scorecard and miss log

Print both on Day 1, before the first diagnostic question.

[scorecard.md](scorecard.md) is the page the whole book writes onto. Seven domain rows, a column per set, each domain's 75% line filled in. A bottom row for each mock's total and zone, with this book's readiness band and every 75% line under it. Print it in landscape at 75% scale: at full size, a Letter page cuts off the last column. The exam facts card follows, for the back. Tick each line once you've checked it against the official exam guide.

[miss-log.md](miss-log.md) is the book's "one page per domain": twelve rows for each, headed with the trap types its drill chapter has you log. Every miss and every "?" gets a row. The Domain 2 section opens with a line for your Domain 2 trap. Appendix C's Playbook map comes last, for a miss that turns out to be a concept you never learned.

## Done when

> You have a blank scorecard with seven named domain rows, a 75% line on each, a mock-trend row with the band drawn as three zones, and an empty miss log.

That's the first half of Chapter 2's checkpoint (ch02:142).

## Where they go next

The scorecard fills left to right: the Diagnostic column from Chapter 3, Drills from Chapters 4 to 7, a mock column a day from Chapter 8 to Appendix A. Chapter 12 reads your verdict off it. The miss log feeds your [tie-break card](../10-mock-3/tie-break-card.md) after Mock 3 and your [trap list](../11-mock-4/trap-list.md) after Mock 4.
