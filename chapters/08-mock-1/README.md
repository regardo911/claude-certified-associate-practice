# Chapter 8 · Mock 1

[answer-grid.md](answer-grid.md) is the one answer grid for all six mocks: the pacing strip and the three rules on top, then 60 lines in four blocks of 15, each block headed with the minute you should be past it. Print a fresh copy for every mock and write the mock's number at the top.

Print the grid before you start the 120-minute timer. Print [score-by-domain.md](score-by-domain.md) only once all 60 are marked. It lists which question belongs to which domain, and as Chapter 8 puts it, "The questions don't show their domain. The key does." (ch08:30)

Done when: "All 60 answered inside 120 minutes, a score for each of the seven domains on your scorecard, and every miss logged to its domain." (ch08:1092)

The seven scores go in the scorecard's Mock 1 column, the total in its bottom row with the zone, and every miss on its domain's page of the miss log.
