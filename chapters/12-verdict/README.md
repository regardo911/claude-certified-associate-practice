# Chapter 12 · The verdict

Day 14, once Mock 6 is marked and on your scorecard. Print two sheets.

## What you fill in

[verdict.md](verdict.md): add each domain's Mock 5 and Mock 6 scores and set the sum against its combined line. The rule and the three verdicts turn that into go, re-check or move, and the last table takes both totals, the domains under their line, the verdict and its date.

[exam-morning-card.md](exam-morning-card.md): eight checks, each with a Done box, ticked for the way you'll sit the exam.

## Success looks like

> A verdict with a date attached, and the exam-morning checklist completed for the way you'll sit the exam. (ch12:115)

## Where they go

A go keeps your booked date. A re-check names the two re-sits and the day you'll decide. A move needs the new date and the latest moment to move the old one, 48 hours before your appointment. Then back to the drill sheets in Chapters 4 to 7, logged misses only. Chapter 12 also has you choose your next credential, or decide you're done for now.
