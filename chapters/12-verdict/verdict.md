Chapter 12 · the verdict sheet (ch12:7-22, 30-34, 48-54, 111)

There is no official readiness standard. The exam guide says plainly that no resource guarantees a pass. So this is my rule, applied to your record, and it's built to be hard to fool.

**Go on your date if both of these are true:**

1. **Mock 5 and Mock 6 are each 44 or more.** Two totals in a row on or above this book's readiness line, on fresh questions, not one good day.
2. **Every domain is at 75% or more across Mocks 5 and 6 combined.** Combined, because one 6-question domain on one mock moves 17 points with a single answer. Two mocks give each domain enough questions to mean something.

**The three verdicts:**

- **Go on the date.** Both totals 44 or more, every domain line met.
- **Re-check, then decide.** You missed by a small margin (one total at 42 or 43, or one domain one question short) and your exam is at least three days away. Re-drill that domain's logged misses, then re-sit the two mocks it scored lowest on. A re-sit scores higher from memory, so it's weaker evidence: go only if both re-sits reach 48 and the domain line is met.
- **Move the date.** Anything else.
