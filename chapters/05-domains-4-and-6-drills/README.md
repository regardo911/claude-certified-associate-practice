# Chapter 5 · Domains 4 and 6 drills

[drill-sheet.md](drill-sheet.md) holds two sets: 34 Domain 4 drills in 68 minutes, then 32 Domain 6 drills in 64, each followed by its own score table. Print it before you sit the first of the two.

## What done looks like

> 26 of 34 or better on Domain 4 and 24 of 32 or better on Domain 6, each on its own, and for every Domain 6 miss you can name which rule decided it: qualified human review before it reaches the person, disclosure of AI use, or the data policy. (ch05:1207)

Domain 4 misses go on the Domain 4 page of your miss log, by lane. Domain 6 misses go on the Domain 6 page, by rule. The two scores go in the scorecard's Drills column on separate rows, never averaged.
