# Chapter 4 · Domain 2 drills

Domain 2 is 44 drills in two sittings of 22, 44 minutes each. [drill-sheet.md](drill-sheet.md) carries both: a line per drill for your letter, the "?" and the mark, Chapter 4's own log row for the break between sittings, and the Domain 2 score table at the end.

Print it before Sitting 1. Score Sitting 1 out of 22 (the line is 17), log every miss and every "?" with its trap type, read the log, and only then sit Sitting 2.

Chapter 4 calls it done at "17 of 22 or better on Sitting 2, and 33 of 44 or better across both sittings, with a trap type written beside every miss" (ch04:828).

Both sitting scores and the total out of 44 go in the scorecard's Drills column for Domain 2. The trap type with the most misses goes at the top of your Domain 2 miss-log page.
