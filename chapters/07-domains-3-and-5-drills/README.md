# Chapter 7 · Domains 3 and 5 drills

Two sets of 25, 50 minutes each. The Domain 3 half of [drill-sheet.md](drill-sheet.md) ends in a wide column for the cheapest model that still does the job, and one sentence saying why. Print it before the Domain 3 sitting.

## You're done when

> 19 of 25 or better on Domain 3 and 19 of 25 or better on Domain 5, each on its own, and you can defend the cheapest model that still does the job for every Domain 3 drill. (ch07:923)

## After it

Log each miss as a size error, a surface error or a configuration error, on its domain's page. With these two scores in, every row of the scorecard's Drills column is filled, and Day 8 is [Mock 1](../08-mock-1/).
