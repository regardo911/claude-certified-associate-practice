Six drill days for seven domains means two domains share a day. Pair your two smallest weighted gaps.

**The weekend version.** Saturday morning: the diagnostic. Saturday afternoon and evening: the drills for your two biggest weighted gaps. Sunday: Mock 1 in the morning, Mock 3 in the afternoon, then the Chapter 12 rule applied to those two in place of Mocks 5 and 6. Two mocks are a thinner record than six, so trust a go from those two less than a go from the full plan. By Sunday you are usually inside the 48-hour line, so moving the date costs the fee.

**The 30-day version.** Day 1: the diagnostic. Days 2 to 19: one domain every three days in your drill order, with your two smallest weighted gaps sharing three days. Days 20 to 25: the six mocks, one a day. Day 26: the verdict, four days before the exam.

Count backwards from your exam date and write a date beside every row. On the two-week and 30-day versions, put the verdict day at least three days before the exam. Moving the date is free only if you do it at least 48 hours ahead, and a re-check needs a day of its own (Chapter 12). If the dates don't fit, cut drill days for your strongest domains, never the mocks.
