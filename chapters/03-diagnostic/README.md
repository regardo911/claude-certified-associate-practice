# Chapter 3 · The diagnostic

Day 1, after the scorecard: 35 questions in 70 minutes. Print one sheet before and two after.

Before the clock starts, print [answer-sheet.md](answer-sheet.md): a line per question with room for two letters, a box for the "?" and one for your mark. It shows no domains, because the diagnostic doesn't either: "none of them tells you its domain. That's on purpose." (ch03:35)

Once all 35 are marked, print two more. [score-by-domain.md](score-by-domain.md) lists which questions belong to which domain. [drill-order-and-calendar.md](drill-order-and-calendar.md) turns your seven scores into weighted gaps (weights filled in), a drill order and a dated calendar. The weekend and 30-day versions sit under the two-week one.

## The checkpoint

> All seven domains have a score out of 5 and a place in your drill order, and the domain with the biggest weighted gap is on Day 2 of your calendar. (ch03:711)

## Then

The seven scores go in the scorecard's Diagnostic column, every miss and "?" in the miss log. Day 2 opens the drill folder at the top of your order: Domain 2 is [Chapter 4](../04-domain-2-drills/), Domains 4 and 6 [Chapter 5](../05-domains-4-and-6-drills/), 1 and 7 [Chapter 6](../06-domains-1-and-7-drills/), 3 and 5 [Chapter 7](../07-domains-3-and-5-drills/).
